# openGym

I coach a small strength group, a few dozen people. We use openGym to keep track of what we lifted, put plans together and jot down weigh-ins. Most of us do that on our phones, and a few people like logging sets from their watches. I run our copy on a little server and I’m the admin, so I’m usually the one getting people set up.

I didn’t build openGym and I’m not involved in developing it. Mostly I go by what I run into using it myself and what people tell me between sets. If someone has a good suggestion, I’ll pass it on. I can tell you what I’d like to happen, but how to get there is up to you.
