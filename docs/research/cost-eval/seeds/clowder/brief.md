# Clowder

A cozy terminal incremental game about raising a thriving clowder of kittens, written in Rust with ratatui. You play it entirely from the keyboard, and it's built to live in a tmux pane for weeks. Nothing in Clowder ever dies, and nothing is ever sacrificed.

You can close the game whenever you like; on reopening it catches up on the time you were away. The game saves to your platform's data directory.

I own it and play it, and I steer it lightly: I say what I'd like as a player, and I'm happy to be surprised by how it turns out, as long as it stays cozy and never punishes the player.
